# Chunk 20: every line the audit of 2026-11-17 added or changed

This is every line a player can see or hear that the audit of 2026-11-17 (chunk 20) added or changed, in one place
for the owner, grouped by where the player meets it, with the words 0.9.2 had and the words the game has now. If you
want a line changed, say which and how: each change is a small follow-up (ADR-0056 decision 2). The first half of the
audit's wording sweep (J1 to J20) is still being finished on its own branch, so its lines aren't here yet; they will
be added when it merges.

How to read it:

- "Now" is copied from the code on `next` at `08e734b`, character for character. A word in braces stands for what
  the game fills in: `{name}` an animal's name, `{tank}` a tank's name, `{n}` a number, `{price}` an amount of money.
  A line with several forms (one or many, a phone or a computer) shows them one under the other or joined by " / ".
- "Before" is what 0.9.2 showed; *new* means there was no line there, and *removed* in "Now" means the line is gone.
- **Answer n** marks a line that builds one of the owner's ten answers in ADR-0056. The letters and numbers in
  brackets are the audit's finding ids (A to J), for whoever makes a change.
- A line about the same thing can appear in two groups when the player meets it in two places.
